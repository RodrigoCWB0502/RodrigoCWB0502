## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a 21-year-old **Computer Science student at PUCPR** (8th semester) from Curitiba, Brazil, passionate about technology for the past 3 years. I enjoy challenges that bring together **Web development, Data and Artificial Intelligence**.

Today I'm a **Data Engineering intern at ACL**, where I build **end-to-end pipelines in Python**, from data consumption to storage, orchestrated with **Apache Airflow** and processing large volumes of data in **SQL Server** and **MongoDB**. I also helped train an **AI model built for municipalities**.

On the web side, I work with **React, Next.js and TypeScript** on the front-end and **Node.js** on the back-end. At **SENAC PR** I built from scratch a **service booking system that is live in production**. To keep growing on the back-end, I'm currently studying **C# and Node.js** at Rocketseat.

My goal is to ship **data-driven, AI-powered products**, with a strong focus on data quality, performance and scalability.
